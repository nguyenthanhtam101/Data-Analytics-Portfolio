## 🚀 Portfolio Projects

### 1. Olist E-commerce Customer Segmentation (RFM)
**Goal:** To classify 100,000+ customers into distinct behavioral segments to optimize marketing ROI and improve retention.

* **Business Problem:** The business was overly dependent on one-time buyers (Standard customers), leading to high acquisition costs and unstable revenue.
* **Solution:** Engineered a 5-stage MongoDB pipeline to merge disparate data and applied RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) modeling to identify high-value vs. at-risk users.
* **Key Insights:** * Discovered that **80% of revenue** stemmed from one-time buyers, indicating a critical need for a loyalty program.
    * Identified a 5% "Champions" group that contributes significantly more to total profit than their size suggests.
* **Technology:** MongoDB Compass, Microsoft Excel, RFM Modeling.
